Clinical Red Flags

Emergency screening criteria — refer immediately if present

CRITICAL

Cauda Equina Syndrome (CES)

  • Bilateral radiculopathy
  • Saddle / perianal numbness
  • Urinary retention or overflow incontinence
  • Loss of anal sphincter tone
  • Bowel dysfunction / faecal incontinence
URGENT

Cervical Spondylotic Myelopathy

  • Bilateral upper limb paraesthesias
  • Hand clumsiness / loss of dexterity
  • Gait ataxia or unsteadiness
  • Positive Hoffman's sign
  • Inverted supinator / Babinski reflex
ALERT

Spinal Malignancy

  • Age > 50
  • Personal history of cancer
  • Unexplained / progressive weight loss
  • Constant, non-mechanical night pain (not posture-dependent)
  • Thoracic spine localised percussion tenderness
ALERT

Spinal Infection

  • Fever / chills / rigors
  • Recent spinal surgery or injection
  • History of IV drug use
  • Systemic illness / immunosuppression
VASCULAR

Vascular — CAD / VBI

5 Ds

  • Dizziness (vertigo / dysequilibrium)
  • Diplopia (double vision)
  • Dysarthria (slurred speech)
  • Dysphagia (difficulty swallowing)
  • Drop attacks (sudden loss of tone)

3 Ns

  • Nausea (sudden onset)
  • Numbness (face or upper limb)
  • Nystagmus

Immediate onward referral is required if any red flag is identified. Document all findings clearly.
